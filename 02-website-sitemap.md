FAQs live as a section on Contact and Process, and as a footer link to `/contact#faqs`. [CONFIRM: separate /faqs page not required]

## Navigation
**Primary:** Solutions, Projects, Process, About, **Talk to us** (button, always visible)
**Secondary / footer:** FAQs, catalogue download [CONFIRM], social links [CONFIRM], careers [CONFIRM], Privacy, Terms
**Mobile:** full-screen menu; floating Talk to us button (call / WhatsApp / callback)

## User journey
1. A developer or project lead lands (search, referral, or direct).
2. Sees scale and proof immediately: 200+ projects, PAN-India, 1-year warranty.
3. Checks relevant projects and the solution that fits.
4. Understands the process and what Dreamz takes responsibility for.
5. Starts a conversation: call the AI-answered number, WhatsApp, or request a callback.

## Page hierarchy (what each page must do)
| Page | Job | Primary CTA |
|---|---|---|
| Home | Build trust fast, route to the right solution and to contact | Talk to us |
| Solutions (index) | Help visitors pick a path | Open a solution page |
| Solution pages | Explain scope, fit and how it works | Request a quote |
| Projects | Prove the claim with real work | Open a case study |
| Case study | Show brief, challenge, action, outcome | Talk to us about a similar project |
| Process | Show what Dreamz handles | Talk to us |
| About | Explain philosophy and accountability | Talk to us |
| Contact | Make starting effortless | Call, WhatsApp or callback |

## Calls to action and conversion paths
- Persistent **Talk to us** in header and floating on mobile
- Quote/callback form on every Solutions and Projects page
- End-of-page CTA band on every page: "Tell us the brief. We'll take it from here."
- Every case study ends with a CTA
- Contact options everywhere: **Call** (AI-answered, `tel:` on mobile), **WhatsApp** (`wa.me` with prefilled message), **Request a callback** (form)
- Desktop: show the number plus the callback form (tapping a number does nothing on desktop)
