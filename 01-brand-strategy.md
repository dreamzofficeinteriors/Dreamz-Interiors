# 01. Brand Strategy

## Business context
- **Company:** Dreamz (commonly referred to as Dreamz Interiors)
- **Delivers:** Commercial, modular and turnkey furniture and workspace solutions across India
- **Track record:** 200+ projects completed
- **Primary focus:** Commercial furniture
- **Main clients:** Mostly real estate developers and project teams; also corporate occupiers and designers/architects who specify furniture
- **Operating model:** No in-house manufacturing. Dreamz specifies, sources, delivers and assembles, and stays accountable for the result. [CONFIRM: "specify and source" wording is accurate]
- **Delivery:** Mostly on-time delivery and assembly
- **Warranty:** 1 year. Water damage and fire damage are not covered.
- **Logo:** Orange and silver. No existing tagline.

## Brand philosophy and identity
**Your Vision, Our Responsibility.**
The client brings the purpose for the space. Dreamz owns everything between the idea and the finished floor: the thinking, the delivery and the follow-through.

## Positioning
Dreamz is the accountable partner for commercial workspaces across India, covering furniture, modular and turnkey, with 200+ projects delivered.

## Brand promise
One team owns your workspace from brief to handover, so you don't have to chase anyone.

## Tagline
- Primary line: **Your Vision, Our Responsibility.** No separate tagline; this line does the job.
- Descriptor (small spaces only: browser title, meta description, loader): *Commercial, Modular and Turnkey Workspace Solutions.*

## Target customer segments
1. **Real estate developers and project teams** (primary)
2. **Corporate occupiers and offices**
3. **Interior designers and architects** who specify furniture

## Core differentiators (only these may be claimed)
- Commercial focus
- 200+ projects completed
- PAN-India delivery
- One accountable team across furniture, modular and turnkey
- A clear 1-year warranty (water and fire damage not covered)

## Brand personality: "The Thoughtful Authority"
A brand that thinks ahead, communicates clearly and takes ownership.

| Trait | Meaning | How it shows on the website |
|---|---|---|
| Intelligent | Demonstrates understanding rather than making claims | Case studies state the brief, constraint and decision |
| Thoughtful | Considers the people, space and purpose behind every decision | Copy names who a space is for and why it is designed that way |
| Confident | Conviction without exaggeration | Short declarative sentences; numbers instead of superlatives |
| Proactive | Anticipates requirements | Process page lists what Dreamz handles before the client asks |
| Dependable | Accountability and follow-through | Visible commitments: schedule, single contact, warranty |
| Approachable | Makes complex workspace decisions feel simpler | Plain language; three clear solution lines; easy ways to start |

## Claims policy
**Allowed:** the facts listed above.
**Not allowed:** in-house manufacturing; "guaranteed on-time" or any on-time percentage unless real data is supplied; awards, certifications, client names/logos, testimonials, prices or timelines unless supplied; superlatives ("best", "world-class").
