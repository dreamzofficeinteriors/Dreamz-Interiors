# 05. Brand Voice

## Tone
**Bold, confident, ownership-driven.** Clear, direct and calm. Conviction without exaggeration.

## Personality in practice: The Thoughtful Authority
Intelligent, Thoughtful, Confident, Proactive, Dependable, Approachable. See 01-brand-strategy.md for how each trait shows up.

## Writing rules
1. Lead with ownership: "We handle", "We deliver", "We stay accountable".
2. Prove, don't praise. "200+ projects delivered across India", not "the best in the industry".
3. Short sentences. Active verbs. No filler.
4. No exclamation marks.
5. Explain complex workspace decisions in plain words.
6. Name the people, space and purpose behind a decision.

## Vocabulary
**Use:** responsibility, ownership, deliver, handover, end-to-end, accountable, planned, on-site, workspace, brief, spec, timeline.
**Avoid:** dream home, luxury, stunning, best-in-class, world-class, cutting-edge, premium, affordable, "one-stop shop", unexplained jargon.

## Before and after
| Weak | On-brand |
|---|---|
| We are the best workspace provider in India! | 200+ projects delivered across India. |
| One-stop shop for all your furniture needs | One team from brief to handover. |
| We guarantee on-time delivery | We set a delivery and assembly schedule at the start and keep you updated through handover. |
| We make our own furniture | We specify, source, deliver and assemble, and we stay accountable for the result. |

## Approved lines
- Hero: **Your vision. Our responsibility.**
- Statement: *We don't hand over furniture. We hand over a workspace that works.*
- Process: *Before you ask, we've checked the site, the specs and the timeline.*
- CTA: *Tell us the brief. We'll take it from here.*

## Sensitive claims, exact wording
- **Warranty:** "1-year warranty. Water and fire damage are not covered." (Full terms to be reviewed by the business or legal.)
- **Delivery:** commit to a schedule and updates; never guarantee.
- **Manufacturing:** never claim; say "specify, source, deliver and assemble".

## AI call agent voice
Same personality, shorter sentences, honest about being an AI.
Greeting: "Hi, I'm Dreamz's AI assistant. Tell me about your workspace, and I'll make sure the right designer takes it from here."
Never quotes firm prices or promises deadlines; says a designer will confirm. Offers a human handoff.
